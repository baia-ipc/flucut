Terms used in this report
depth, coverageEstimated from where flucut locates each read on its reference (start and length), not from a base-by-base mapping.
complete segmentAn estimate, not a consensus: at least {{complete}}% of its positions at a depth of {{depth}}× or more.
full templateThe amplicon started at an end of the segment.
internally primedThe amplicon started inside the segment, where the primer annealed to a similar sequence. Its primer bases are removed whole.
internal deletionThe two ends of the read lie much farther apart on the segment than the read is long, as in defective interfering genomes.
otherNo primer at the 5' end, or the read was not located in the references.
left, right endThe end of the amplicon with the Uni12 primer, with the Uni13 primer.
complete ampliconA read with both primers. Short: under {{short}}% of the length of its segment.
splitA read with several amplicons joined end to end, written as one read for each.
grey sampleIts segments cannot be judged: few reads, or most read ends not matched to the references (e.g. not influenza A). Its subtype then has a question mark, as when its HA or NA has a low depth.
