Split into several amplicons: reads which contained two or more amplicons joined end to end, split at the junctions; each part is then counted as a read in the other lines. Both primers, one primer, no primer: the primers found at the ends of the reads (within the window of each end), in either orientation. Primer dimers: both primers and nothing between them. Too short: empty or shorter than the minimum length after the trimming (dimers included), not written. Minus strand: reads whose primers were found on their reverse complement (they are written in their original orientation). Adapter at the start, at the end: a Nanopore adapter removed at an end of the read at which no primer was found.
