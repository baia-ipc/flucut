About
=====

flucut is developed by Giorgio Gonnella at BAIA, Institut Pasteur du
Cambodge. It is free software under the ISC license (``LICENSE``).

The source code: https://github.com/baia-ipc/flucut

flucut includes libcbaia (ISC), edlib (MIT), libyaml (MIT), zlib (zlib
license) and libdeflate (MIT), in ``ext``, each with its license.

Use of AI tools
---------------

The code, the documentation, the benchmark and this manual were written
with the help of an AI assistant, under the direction and review of the
author. The logo and the illustration of the title page were made with AI
image tools.
