Glossary
========

amplicon
   A molecule made by the PCR: here a segment, or part of it, between
   the two primers.

complete amplicon
   A read in which the primers of both ends were found.

conserved terminus
   The 12 or 13 bases at the ends of the influenza A segments, the same
   in all segments and subtypes, to which the universal primers bind.

flank
   The bases of a read outside the primers: adapter, barcode, and other
   bases of the library preparation.

internal priming
   The start of an amplicon by a primer annealed at an interior position
   of a segment, whose bases then replace those of the template in the
   reads.

probe
   The bases of a read after the primer of an end, located in the
   references to classify the end.

scheme
   The primers expected at the two ends of an amplicon.

tail
   The bases of a primer before its conserved terminus, added by the
   protocol (e.g. ``ACGCGTGATCAG`` of the mbtuni primers).

window
   The part of a read, at each end, in which the primer of that end is
   accepted (``-w``).
