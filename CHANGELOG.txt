== unreleased ==

- the universal primers of the influenza A multisegment RT-PCR
  (Uni12/Uni13), built in: the conserved termini of the segments and the
  tailed primers of the protocols, each with the source of its sequence;
  a primer is stored as the whole oligonucleotide with the offset of its
  conserved terminus, as the tail and the terminus overlap in the
  published primers
- search of a primer in a read: alignment with errors, at the ends of
  the read also partial (a read can begin inside a primer), with the
  degenerate bases of the primers (e.g. the two forms of the Uni12
  primer) and of the reads; the bounds of the conserved terminus in the
  read are computed from the alignment path
- primer schemes: the primers expected at each end of an amplicon,
  built-in "mbt" (the tailed primers, the default) and "uni" (the
  conserved termini alone)
- internal priming: a primer is accepted as the primer of an end only
  within a window of that end of the read (default 100 bases), as a
  match deeper inside the read is not the primer which started the
  amplicon and trimming there would remove template; the matches
  between the two end primers are reported as internal matches (see
  docs/internal-priming.md)
- primer schemes: "uni12-mbt13" (the untailed degenerate Uni12 primer
  and the tailed Uni13 primer) is the default
- analysis of a read and trimming: both ends and both orientations, the
  reads are not reverse-complemented; the policy "primer" (the
  default) removes all the primer-derived bases, which also removes the
  internal priming artefact without references; "terminus" keeps the
  bases of the conserved termini
- reference sequences: FASTA reader; scripts/fetch_references.py
  downloads the segment references from NCBI with their provenance
- scripts/fetch_references.py: segments 7 (MP) and 8 (NS) are searched
  by the names of their genes (M1/M2, NS1/NEP/NS2), as NCBI annotates
  them, instead of MP[Gene] and NS[Gene], which matched no record: with
  --segment all, they were silently left out; test of the queries in
  make test (tests/test-fetch-references.py, --live for the download)
- scripts/fetch_references.py: a request answered by HTTP 429 (too many
  requests) is retried up to 5 times, with exponential backoff (1, 2, 4,
  8, 16 s) or the wait of the Retry-After header if longer (at most
  60 s), instead of stopping the download
- classification of a read against the references: the bases after the
  primer of each end are located in the segments (both strands, with
  errors and IUPAC codes); at a terminus the read is a normal amplicon,
  at an interior position it was internally primed; ambiguous reads are
  classified as terminal
- with the policy "terminus", the trimming is decided per read: the
  conserved termini of the normal amplicons are kept (only tails are
  removed), the whole primer of an internally primed end is removed;
  ends which cannot be located in the references are reported as
  unclassified; the references are then required (with the default
  policy they are optional and measure internal priming)
